Remz IPTV Player

Privacy Policy

Your media. Your privacy.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Remz IPTV Player ("REMZ", "we" or "the app") handles information when you use the app. REMZ is a media player that lets you connect your own M3U playlists and Xtream-compatible services. REMZ does not provide, host, sell or distribute television channels, films or other media content. You are responsible for connecting sources you are authorised to use.

1. Who is responsible?

REMZ is developed and operated by Filip Granström, who is the app operator and privacy contact for this service. For privacy questions or requests, contact: remzIPTV@proton.me.

2. Information handled by REMZ

Depending on the features you use, information handled on your device may include:

  • Media source details: M3U playlist addresses, imported playlist contents, XMLTV / EPG addresses, and Xtream server addresses, usernames and passwords.
  • Library information: channel, film and series names, categories, programme information, artwork addresses and other metadata supplied by your source.
  • App data: favourites, preferences, selected source, recently viewed items, playback positions and viewing history.
  • Local files: when you choose an M3U file through the device file picker, REMZ reads that selected file to import its contents.

REMZ does not require you to create a REMZ account.

3. How information is stored

REMZ is designed to store your library and preferences locally on your device rather than in a REMZ-operated cloud account.

  • Xtream server credentials are stored using the device platform's secure storage facility.
  • M3U source addresses and stream links are stored in a locally encrypted data file. The encryption key is kept in secure device storage.
  • Other app information, such as favourites, settings and playback history, is stored in local app storage.

The app does not provide cloud synchronisation for this information. Removing app data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information; keep your own copy of any source details you need.

4. Connections to media providers and other services

When you add or use a source, REMZ connects directly to the server or service you specify. These connections are necessary to download playlists, retrieve programme-guide information or metadata, check a connection when you request diagnostics, and play media.

The service you connect to may receive your IP address, network/request information, the requested media or programme information, and the credentials or playlist address needed to provide the service. For Xtream connections, your username and password are sent to the provider's server as part of its authentication requests.

REMZ also includes a sample film for demonstration purposes. Playing it connects to the external media host that serves the sample. Third-party services operate independently and are responsible for their own privacy practices. Review the relevant provider's terms and privacy policy before connecting.

5. Network security

REMZ supports HTTPS and HTTP sources. HTTPS encrypts information in transit. HTTP does not. If you use an HTTP provider, playlist addresses, stream links, credentials and requests may be visible to others on the network. REMZ displays warnings for relevant HTTP connections, but cannot make an external provider's server secure. Use HTTPS whenever your provider supports it.

6. Analytics, advertising, website hosting and sharing

The app version covered by this policy does not include a dedicated in-app advertising or analytics SDK. REMZ does not operate a server that receives your playlists, credentials, favourites or viewing history, and does not sell this information. Information is sent to the external media services you choose to connect to when required for the requested functionality.

The public version of this Privacy Policy is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit that page, GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to operate and secure its hosting service, in accordance with GitHub's own privacy terms. The policy page does not intentionally use analytics scripts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stored by REMZ remains on your device until you remove the relevant source or clear the app's local data. You can use Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear all local data to remove locally stored playlists, credentials, favourites, preferences and viewing history. This does not cancel subscriptions or accounts with external providers.

8. Your rights and privacy requests

Depending on applicable data protection law and the circumstances of the processing, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Most library and account information handled by REMZ is stored locally on your device and is not available to the app operator. For questions about information the app operator may handle, use the contact details below. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's privacy

REMZ is a general-purpose media player and does not provide a child account system. The app operator does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a REMZ account service.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be made available in the app and at the public Privacy Policy web address listed in the app's store information.
